# Generic shipment model

CAP-22 treats shipment tracking as the core workflow and landed-cost/customs calculation as an optional capability attached to orders and eligible cross-border shipments.

## Transport modes

- `PARCEL`: domestic or cross-border courier parcels.
- `ROAD`: road freight and line-haul movements.
- `AIR`: air cargo, with optional origin/destination airports and customs gateways.
- `OCEAN`: container/LCL/FCL freight, with optional ports of loading/discharge.
- `RAIL`: rail freight and terminals.
- `MULTIMODAL`: a shipment with multiple planned legs or gateways.

## Location rules

Store exact origin and destination delivery addresses when known. A carrier chooses and operates the route; the application stores the commercial endpoints, optional planned gateways, and carrier-reported events. Ports and airports are optional and should not be required for a domestic parcel. `logistics_locations` is a reusable catalog for warehouses, airports, seaports, rail terminals, and customs facilities. `shipment_stops` represents planned or operational milestones, while normalized append-only carrier events remain the evidence of actual movement.

## Customer identity

Customer UUIDs and human-readable customer numbers are generated by the API. Clients must not submit IDs on create. `orders.customer_id` is nullable to preserve historical rows; `customer_ref` remains for legacy/external references. Do not delete customers with historical orders; deactivate them instead.

## Current increment and remaining API/UI work

Migration V11 creates the customer master, nullable order link, logistics-location catalog, shipment transport-mode metadata, endpoint address columns, and ordered shipment stops. Customer create/list/detail/update endpoints and a Customers console section are implemented. Full location/stop CRUD and shipment form integration are still pending; do not treat the schema foundation as a complete logistics execution module.
